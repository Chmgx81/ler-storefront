# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people. Critique the change, not the author.
- Assume good faith, and ask a question before assuming a motive.
- Accept that "this does not fit the project's scope" is a valid answer.
- Give credit for ideas and work that came from someone else.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time; a slow reply is not a rejection.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing a report of a problem because you have not experienced it.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the
maintainers through GitHub — open a private report from the repository's
**Security** tab, or message a maintainer directly on GitHub. Do not open a
public issue.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, keep the reporter's
identity confidential where possible, and may respond with a private warning, a
request to edit or delete a comment, a temporary block from the repository, or a
permanent ban. Maintainers who are themselves involved in an incident will be
excluded from handling it.
